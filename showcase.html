Replay a real run of the attack search on the small materials ontology, one step at a time: the impact of each element, greedy search, and the bandit's search tree.

What this is

A recording of the library, not a simulation. experiments/walkthrough/trace.py runs Aegis's own impact ranking, greedy search, UCT, and beam search on the materials ontology of the playground, and writes every step down; this page replays it. Under the ontology, AFF is the affected area of the state shown, as forensics.affected_area computes it (after Fan et al., SIGMOD 2011): the answers each question lost and gained.
