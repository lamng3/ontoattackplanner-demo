Attack a knowledge graph through its ontology

The knowledge graph never changes. The attacker edits only the ontology the pipeline reasons with, and the answers drift. A stealthy attacker leaves every term the questions name in place, so every query still validates.

    Each attacker searches on its own model (a pseudo-KG sampled through a guarded retriever, a surrogate reasoner, or only the answers it can probe), and its attack is then run on the real pipeline. The searches combine edits that hurt on their own, so they can miss a pair that only hurts together; a grey-box attacker can stumble on one the white box missed.

    Drift as edits pile up

    random edits, 5 orders

    Answer drift is the mean Jaccard distance from the honest answers. Failure loses answers, pollution adds wrong ones, drift moves things that still look right.
